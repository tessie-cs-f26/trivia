Chronology

Build a timeline of 12 historical events. Place each new event where you think it belongs.

How to play
  • Your first event is placed for you, with its date showing.
  • Each turn you get a new event with no date. Tap the gap in the timeline where it belongs.
  • Then its date is revealed. If it’s in the right place, keep going. If not, the game ends.
  • Your score is the number of events in your timeline. Reach 12 for a perfect game.
