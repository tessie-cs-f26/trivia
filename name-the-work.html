Name the Work

Characters appear one per second, from minor to major. Name the novel, play, or poem as soon as you know it.

How it works
  • A new character appears every second, from minor to major.
  • Characters named in the title are never shown. When a work has one clear main character who isn’t named in the title, they appear last, marked with a ★.
  • You can answer at any time, and in a timed round, answering early leaves more time for other novels.
  • The six choices come from the same tradition when possible: American, British and Irish, or world literature.
