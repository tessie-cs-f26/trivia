Geography Trivia

World capitals, U.S. state capitals, and state nicknames, asked in both directions.

How it works
  • Each question goes one of two ways at random: name the capital (or nickname), or name the country or state.
  • When you’re asked for a capital, one wrong choice is the largest city that isn’t the capital, like Lagos for Nigeria or Chicago for Illinois. It’s left out when the largest city isn’t clear.
  • Where a country’s capital is debated or split, the question says which one it means, like Bolivia’s constitutional capital.
  • Wrong country choices come from the same continent when possible.
