ICC Recurring Concepts

Questions from your ICC deck whose answers come up in at least three different seasons, as an answer line or inside a clue.

How it works
  • 920 recurring concepts across 1,490 cards from Seasons 1 to 11. Concepts that show up in only one or two seasons, and very broad ones like countries or “history,” are left out.
  • Working on quizzes the cards you haven’t suspended. Already known quizzes your suspended cards. All mixes both.
  • Each round is 20 fill-in-the-blank questions. Spelling slips, missing accents and leading “the” are forgiven, any “or” or “accept” answer on the card counts, and a last name is enough for a person.
  • If the game marks you wrong but your answer was fine, press I was right to count it.
