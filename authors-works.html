Authors & Works

Name the author of a famous work, or pick the work from its author and a one-line summary.

How it works
  • Author questions show a title. Pick who wrote it. Wrong choices are usually writers from the same tradition: American, British and Irish, or world literature.
  • Work questions show an author and a one-sentence summary. Wrong choices include other works by the same author when there are any, so read the summary carefully.
  • Summaries never use the title’s words. A character named in the title is called “the title character.”
