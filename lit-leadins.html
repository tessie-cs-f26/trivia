Lit Lead-ins

Quiz bowl lead-in clues appear word by word, hardest first. Name the work as soon as you know it.

How it works
  • Clues come from the MSNCT literature deck, about 190 novels, plays, poems and stories.
  • Words appear five per second, with a short pause before each new clue. Clues start hard and get easier.
  • Buzz in any time by picking one of the six titles. The wrong choices are the same kind of work.
  • Clue order: “Hardest first” shows each work’s clues in the deck’s order. “Random” shuffles them, so an easy giveaway can come up early.
  • Each round is a race of 10 questions: the most right answers wins, and ties go to the faster time.
