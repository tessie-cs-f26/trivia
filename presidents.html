Name the President

Every year from 1789 to 2026. Read the year’s major events and name the president.

Which president counts?
  • If a president began an elected term that year, that president is the answer, even if he died soon after. For 1841, that’s William Henry Harrison, not John Tyler.
  • Otherwise, it’s the president who served the most of that year. For 1923, that’s Warren G. Harding, who died in August.
  • Wrong choices include anyone else who was president that year, plus presidents who served around the same time.
