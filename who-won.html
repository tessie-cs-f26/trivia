Who Won?

U.S. presidential elections from 1796 to 2024. Given the year and a major issue, pick the winner.

How the choices work
  • One wrong choice is the candidate who lost that election.
  • The other two are presidents who served within a few terms of the winner, sometimes both earlier, sometimes both later, sometimes one of each.
  • Elections where the winner ran unopposed (1789, 1792 and 1820) are left out.
