Young Reader Lit

98 children’s and young adult classics, from Goodnight Moon to The Hunger Games. Name the book from its clues, name the author, or match a character to the book.

How it works
  • Books: clues appear word by word, hardest first, ending with a summary of the story. Pick the book as soon as you know it.
  • Authors: who wrote the book?
  • Characters: which book is the character from?
  • Mixed mixes all three. Each question has six choices.
  • Each round is a race of 10 questions: the most right answers wins, and ties go to the faster time.
