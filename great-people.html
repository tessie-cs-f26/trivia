100 Great People

Michael Hart’s 100 most influential people in history. Facts appear word by word, hardest first: name the person as soon as you know.

How it works
  • The people come from Michael Hart’s book The 100: A Ranking of the Most Influential Persons in History (1992 edition).
  • Facts appear five words per second, with a short pause between facts. They get easier as they go: the last fact is what the person is most famous for, usually with their nationality, as in “This Scottish-born inventor patented the telephone.”
  • Pick from six names at any time. The wrong choices usually come from the same field: scientists with scientists, rulers with rulers.
  • Each round is a race of 10 questions: the most right answers wins, and ties go to the faster time.
