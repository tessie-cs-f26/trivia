Middle School Trivia

Quick-fire general knowledge, weighted like NAQT middle school quiz bowl: science, history, literature, geography, the arts, pop culture, current events, and more.

How it works
  • 100 questions, split across topics the way NAQT middle school tournaments are. Pick one topic or play them all.
  • Each round goes through every question in the topic you picked, once each, at your own pace. Each right answer is worth 1 point, and you can post your score to the class leaderboard at the end.
  • Current events questions were last checked in November 2026.
  • Each question has six choices, and the wrong ones are close calls on purpose, like Durmstrang for Hogwarts or Daedalus for Icarus.
